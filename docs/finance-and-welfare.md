# Finance and welfare

[← Back to the Product Storybook](../product-storybook.md)

## Status: explore with clear boundaries

The church wants welfare records to cover more than one-off assistance. The meeting named recurring home/mission support and emergency assistance alongside contributions and support given to people.

## Confirmed direction

- Keep contributions received and assistance provided as distinct records and totals.
- Support may include recurring home or mission commitments and emergency assistance, once their workflow is agreed.
- Individual financial and welfare details need a narrower audience than leadership summaries.
- Corrections must remain traceable rather than silently overwrite history.

## Discovery questions

| Topic                | Decision still needed                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recurring support    | Frequency, start/end conditions, beneficiary/mission linkage, pauses, and reconciliation.               |
| Emergency assistance | Intake, evidence, urgency handling, recording, and follow-up.                                           |
| Reminders            | Whether reminders are useful, whom they reach, and what happens after a missed action.                  |
| Approval             | Who can request, approve, pay, correct, or export; whether a second approval is required.               |
| Privacy              | Which details are visible in summaries, reports, audit logs, and exports; retention and deletion rules. |

## Access boundary

The pilot shared account cannot give person-by-person attribution. Eventual role-based access must separate finance, welfare, pastoral summaries, and any sensitive assistance data. Do not implement reminder or approval mechanics until their owners and permissions are agreed.
