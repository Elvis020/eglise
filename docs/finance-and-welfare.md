# Tithes and welfare

[← Back to the Product Storybook](../product-storybook.md)

## Purpose

Keep tithes, member welfare contributions, and assistance given to members organized, restricted, and reconcilable.

## Confirmed activities

V1 records three distinct activities:

1. Tithes received.
2. Welfare contributions received from members.
3. Assistance given to members.

Contributions received and assistance provided have separate records and totals. Separate welfare case management is outside the current scope.

## A simple finance story

An authorized officer records a contribution, checks the period total, and later corrects an error using a traceable adjustment rather than silently overwriting history.

When assistance is given, the officer records it separately against the relevant person where applicable. This allows authorized staff to reconcile what came in with what support went out.

## Visibility boundary

Individual tithe and welfare records remain linked to each person for authorized finance and welfare staff.

Pastors see summary totals only:

- Total tithes.
- Total welfare contributions.
- Total assistance given.

Pastors do not see who gave, how much a particular person gave, or who received assistance. The same boundary applies to profiles, reports, drill-downs, direct requests, and exports.

## Recordkeeping boundary

V1 records financial activity; it does not collect online payments. Different currencies are never combined into one unlabeled total.

Corrections use adjustments or reversals so the original record remains traceable.
