# Healing and deliverance

[← Back to the Product Storybook](../product-storybook.md)

## Status: sensitive discovery area

The meeting separated two related but fundamentally different workflows. They must not share a broad “care” permission by default.

## Healing and Deliverance School

The school may need to organise:

- Topics or learning units.
- Participant progress and readiness.
- Booking or next-step coordination.

The school workflow is still discovery: define who can see progress, what readiness means, who manages bookings, and what history is useful.

## Healing and Deliverance Clinic

Clinic forms, personal history, and reporting are restricted to designated ministers. This is a separate sensitive area, not a general school, Bible-study, or pastoral-note feature.

Before any clinic implementation, the church must agree the designated-minister list, least-privilege permissions, consent and notice, data minimisation, retention/deletion, corrections, exports, audit visibility, incident handling, and the process for removing access. A shared pilot account must never be used to claim individual attribution for clinic activity.

## Boundary

School progress and clinic history must remain separate records, views, exports, and reports. General leaders, facilitators, or administrators do not receive clinic access merely because they serve in a related ministry.
