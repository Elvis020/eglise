# Discipleship and Word Digest

[← Back to the Product Storybook](../product-storybook.md)

These are V2 feature areas. The church continues using its current processes during V1.

## Discipleship — the care room

### Purpose

Make responsibility for people visible and support consistent follow-up.

### Story

A coordinator assigns a person to a discipler. The discipler records a conversation, outcome, and next action. A pastor can see who is responsible, who has no assignment, and where follow-up is overdue without personally coordinating every interaction.

### What the room may contain

- Disciplers and their assigned people.
- Responsible pastor or coordinator.
- Assignment start, end, and transfer history.
- Follow-up date and outcome.
- Next action and next follow-up date.
- Workload, unassigned people, and overdue follow-up views.

### Boundaries

- Disciples use the shared people directory; there is no separate disciplee database.
- Missing reports are different from a recorded unsuccessful contact.
- Sensitive notes follow an approved access scope.
- Transfers preserve history.

### Open decisions

- Final discipler/disciplee terminology.
- Whether a person can have one primary discipler or several active disciplers.
- Minimum progress-report fields.
- Who may see care notes after reassignment.

## Word Digest — the learning room

### Purpose

Help coordinators and facilitators guide people through Word Digest and understand progress.

### Provisional path

**Experience → Assimilation → Assurances → Doctrine → Growth**

### Story

A learner joins a class and a facilitator records class attendance separately from Sunday service attendance. At the end of the cycle, a coordinator reviews results and decides who progresses.

After the Assimilation process and paperwork are complete, the administrator reviews the cohort and marks eligible people as members together. No separate pastor approval step is required in the application.

### What the room may contain

- Stages, cohorts, groups, facilitators, and class sessions.
- Enrollment history.
- Class attendance.
- Assessment results and completion decisions.
- Reviewed promotion, repeat, exemption, and exception decisions.
- Membership handoff after Assimilation.
- Baptism information where needed.

### Boundaries

- Enrollment is different from class attendance.
- Class attendance is different from main-service attendance.
- A cycle ending does not automatically promote every learner.
- Growth completion does not automatically appoint a facilitator or create staff access.
- Curriculum authoring, online examinations, and automatic certificate generation are separate possibilities.

### Open decisions

- Confirmed stage names and sequence.
- Cycle length and class-group structure.
- Pass, absence, repeat, exemption, and progression rules.
- Certificate fields and issue process.
- Historical placement for existing learners.
