# Discipleship and Bible study

[← Back to the Product Storybook](../product-storybook.md)

## Status: explore together

The earlier Word Digest idea is now a discovery area for Bible study and learning. It should grow from the church's actual material and facilitation practice rather than impose a fixed class sequence.

## Bible study discovery

The meeting identified a possible path in which study materials are derived from sermons. The team will discover:

- How a sermon becomes a study outline or material, who prepares it, and what should remain a Telegram link rather than be copied into Eglise.
- Which facilitators can see, prepare, or update materials.
- Whether participation means enrolment, session attendance, completion, discussion, or another agreed signal.
- How groups, cycles, progression, and historical records should work, if the church needs them.

Bible-study participation is separate from main-service attendance. Materials and learner information need agreed access and retention rules before implementation.

## Discipleship and ordinary care

Discipleship follow-up remains a possible later workflow based on the same people directory. Assignment, notes, responsibility, overdue follow-up, and leader visibility have not been confirmed. The system must not infer lack of care from a missing record.

## Boundaries

- Membership recognition continues to follow the church process; it is not automatically granted by attendance or study participation.
- Do not grant facilitator or staff access because someone completes a class.
- Keep general discipleship information distinct from the Healing and Deliverance Clinic's confidential records. See [Healing and deliverance](healing-and-deliverance.md).

## Open governance

Facilitator permissions, learner access, sensitive-note boundaries, retention, exports, and audit attribution await the eventual role-based model. The shared pilot account is not a substitute for these controls.
